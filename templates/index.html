The 4-Step Resilient Food Workflow

Deterministic backend logic strictly guarantees expiry verification at every stage.

Step 1
📦

Donate

Donors post surplus food details, quantity, pickup location, and verified future expiration date.

→
Step 2
🔍

View

Community members and charities view active available items filtered by category and distance.

→
Step 3
🤝

Claim

Recipients reserve a donation instantly. Backend updates status to Claimed to avoid double collection.

→
Step 4
✨

Collect

Recipient picks up food from the donor and confirms collection. Surplus food successfully saved!
